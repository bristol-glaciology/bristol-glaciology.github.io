---
title: "New BGC ‘Cryoegg’ research tells us more about Greenland’s subglacial drainage system."
excerpt: "CryoEgg Research"
date: 2026-08-24
categories:
  - announcement
---

The ‘Cryoegg’ subglacial wireless probe deployed in several moulins in southwest Greenland (Kalaallit Nunaat) has showed that parts of the drainage system become periodically fragmented and re-connected, even during the peak of the melt season. This behaviour helps us understand the links between melting and ice motion, to better understand how the ice sheet will respond to increased surface melt. This paper is a product of the [NERC-funded ‘SLIDE’ Subglacial Lakes under Isunnguata Sermia: Dynamics and Evolution project](https://the-slide-project.github.io/)

You can read Ryan, Liz, Matt, Tifenn and Tom's featured research in [*AGU Advances*]( https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2026AV002403).
